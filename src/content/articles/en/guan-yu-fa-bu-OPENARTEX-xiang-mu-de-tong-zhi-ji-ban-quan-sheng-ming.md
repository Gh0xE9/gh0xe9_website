In October 2026, the upstream Autumn-27/ARTEX repository was removed from GitHub. The source did not disappear with it—it is scattered across 153 repositories in the form of v0.3.15, renamed, reskinned, fitted with an arsenal, and put back online under the name of an “AI autonomous penetration platform.”

Here, everything that can be found is placed on the register, and certification at levels I–V is granted according to the depth of modification. Certification does not mean endorsement; it only means we have seen you.

# What this is

ARTEX was the winning project of Baidu’s 2026 “agent+” attack-defense challenge: an autonomous penetration-testing system driven by an LLM multi-agent setup, released as open source under AGPL-3.0. In October 2026, the upstream repository Autumn-27/ARTEX was removed from GitHub, and the public source stopped around v0.3.15.

The source did not disappear. **It has continued to spread as forks, stashes, mirrors, Chinese, English, and Korean localizations, reskins, and builds fitted with an arsenal**, growing into more than a hundred repositories within a few weeks. Some of these are only backups, some have been turned into someone else’s product, and some have been used for “attack-defense exercises” that had no premise of authorization.

This site does three things (in a vein similar to the SCP Foundation):

1. Collect (list, as completely as possible, the publicly findable repositories related to ARTEX)
2. Grade (assign levels I–V according to the depth of modification)
3. Leave a record (keep the repository’s self-description and attribution status, for later tracing)

Certification is an act of registration. It is not an honor, and it is not a verdict.
